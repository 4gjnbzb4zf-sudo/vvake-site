What's new

Every VVake release on iPhone, Apple Watch, Android and Wear OS: what was added, improved and fixed.

  1. 1.0.0 (88)

    November 9, 2026BetaiPhone · Apple Watch · Android · Wear OS

    New

    • Beat the clock: set yourself a walk or a run in a time ("5 km in 30:00") from Race your best, your watch, the web app, by voice ("set me 5 km in 30 minutes") or by asking your coach. You see the pace it needs and how it fits your own recent form (with a kinder time to try when it's a big stretch), and you pick even pace, an easy start or a negative split.
    • On the way, your phone and your watch show how many seconds you're ahead or behind, the finish you're heading for and the pace to hold for the rest. Your coach speaks at the start, each km (each half km on short goals), when you drift off pace, for the last km and at the finish, in the coach's recorded voice, even offline.
    • At the end: made or how close, your splits against the plan, retry or a next target, a share card, and your best time per distance.

    Improved

    • Health first: if your heart rate gets very high, or climbs at the same pace (heat, tiredness), the coach tells you to ease off. One tap drops the target and your workout simply goes on.

  2. 1.0.0 (87)

    November 8, 2026BetaiPhone · Apple Watch · Android · Wear OS

    New

    • Watch and phone of different brands (an Apple Watch with an Android phone, a Wear OS watch with an iPhone): your watch now shows today's meals and sends Eaten, Undo, the "How was it?" faces, grocery ticks and a meal you log by voice straight to VVake. Nothing is logged twice, even if it's sent again. With no connection the watch keeps it and says "Saved on your watch"; it goes as soon as you're back online.

    Improved

    • The calendar file you download on vvake.com now has the meals you picked too (with how to cook them and the prep), not only your week plan. A picked meal you replaced with something else shows what you had, and Undo brings it back on the same event.
    • Your phone reads today's meals again when you come back to VVake, so what you did on your watch shows right away.

  3. 1.0.0 (86)

    November 8, 2026BetaiPhone · Apple Watch · Android · Wear OS

    New

    • Undo on your watch: open a meal that shows "Planned: …" on your Apple Watch or Wear OS watch and tap Undo. "Plan back" shows at once; the meal you logged stays logged. Your phone gets it even if it's out of reach for a while.

    Improved

    • The calendar file you download on vvake.com shows what you had instead of a planned meal, with the plan in the notes. After Undo, download it again and the planned meal comes back on the same event.
    • Meals you logged before the last update now show "You had instead" on the planned meal's page too, with Undo. Totals still count each meal once.

  4. 1.0.0 (85)

    November 8, 2026BetaiPhone · Apple Watch · Android · Wear OS

    New

    • Log a meal (photo, typed, search, coach or watch) and it takes the planned meal's place: Today, the week plan, your calendar and your watch show what you really had (foods, about how many kcal), with a check.
    • The planned meal stays as a small "Planned: …" line with Undo, so the plan is never lost. Deleting the meal you logged brings the plan back too.
    • Two planned meals could fit (a plan meal and a pick, or a meal kind you didn't plan)? VVake asks once: "Replace which planned meal?" or "Just log it".

    Improved

    • When a planned meal is replaced, the grocery items only it needed are offered as "Not needed on your list any more?" (Remove from list or Keep them). Nothing is removed on its own.
    • Editing what you logged updates the slot right away. Day and week totals count what you ate once, never the plan on top.
    • "How was it?" only asks about meals you ate as planned.

  5. 1.0.0 (84)

    • Talk and Coach are one place now. The Coach tab sits in the middle of the tab bar with the mic look: open it and the talk is on top (the big mic, live or tap-to-talk, the last lines, the coach's cards, a field to type), with the coach's things below (what it learned, this week's tips, Get to know me, missed calls) and its settings together at the bottom.
    • Talk from anywhere: hold the Coach tab and your coach listens at once. Siri, the app shortcut, the workout notification and vvake://talk open the talk directly; during a workout it stays a small sheet over the workout.

    • On vvake.com/app the same: the Coach tab is the talk (hold it, or double-click it with a mouse, to talk at once).

  8. 1.0.0 (81)

    November 5, 2026BetaiPhone · Android · Apple Watch · Wear OS

    New

    • Edit numbers: on any food of a meal, type the grams, energy (kcal or kJ) and protein, carbs and fat yourself, for example from a label. "1,5" and "1.5" both work. Your numbers are kept exactly as typed.
    • Logged meals can be edited from Nutrition › Today (tap a meal › Edit).
    • Portion shortcuts under each food: ½×, 1×, 1½×, 2×.

    Improved

    • Calories are shown as estimates (≈) on the phone, the watches and the web.
    • When the kcal of a food don't match its protein, carbs and fat, a calm line says so without blocking you (a label can be right).
    • A meal that can't be saved now says what to fix (a number too high, no food yet) or that it's the connection, and the sheet stays open.

    Fixed

    • Logging a meal by voice or from the watch ("Log a meal", the coach) now really saves it in your food log. Before, only the meals habit was ticked. The words are turned into foods and numbers (or kept as words to finish later when they can't be read).
    • After logging a meal, Today's nutrition card shows it right away.
    • Meals logged in the evening could land on the next day for some accounts: the day now follows your phone's time zone.
    • A glass of wine or a beer showed a fraction of its calories (the alcohol wasn't counted): fixed.
    • The web app: renaming a food with a long name made the save fail.

    • Sheets and pop-ups (nutrition options, settings, plans, every screen that slides up): the top is no longer cut by the rounded corners, and long bottom sheets stop below the status bar and scroll.
