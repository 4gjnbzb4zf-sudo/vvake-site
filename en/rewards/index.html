04 / How prizes work

Effort in, prizes out

  1. 01

    VVake Coach Plus funds the pool

    Each month 15% of net VVake Coach Plus subscription revenue goes to an open prize pool contract, with a hash of that month's report on chain.

  2. 02

    The pool turns ETH into VVAKE by rule

    Anyone can trigger the next step, in small capped chunks, at most once per interval. The VVAKE goes straight to the rewards contract, never to a person.

  3. 03

    VVake Coach Plus or the free entry, same prize

    Two ways in: VVake Coach Plus or the free entry (no purchase necessary), same rules and same prizes. A week counts if your account, your entry and your linked wallet existed before the week started, you're 18 or older and allowed to receive prizes where you live (you confirm it when linking), you recorded your sessions with the app on a real iPhone or Apple Watch, you moved with heart-rate data from a watch or a heart strap (at least 3 sessions of 10+ minutes or 90 active minutes, with movement; a phone-only workout gets VV points in the app but scores 0 toward prizes), and you answered the week's skill question, as Canadian law requires for prizes. Move Quests never count toward prizes.

  4. 04

    Points with just your phone, prizes with heart rate

    A walk, run, hike or ride recorded with just your phone gets about 80% of the VV points the same workout would get with heart rate, streak bonus included. It counts when the phone's checks agree: steps that fit the distance, no stretch in a car, bus or train, and the real app on a real phone. Prizes still need heart rate from a watch or a heart strap: phone-only points never count toward them, and money never buys a share of the pool.

  5. 05

    Only the real app counts

    Only sessions recorded with the VVake app on a real iPhone or Apple Watch count toward prizes (the app proves it with Apple's App Attest). Sessions from the web, a simulator or other tools stay in your journal with their points, but don't count toward prizes. One phone or watch counts toward prizes for one account only. Sessions from a device that already counts for another account wait for a review.

  6. 06

    The pilot

    During the pilot: at most 1,000 VVAKE per person and 20,000 VVAKE in all each week, for the first 200 eligible people; others join a waiting list. What a cap leaves stays in the pool for later weeks. A person reviews every week, and nothing is posted until that review is done.

  7. 07

    The weekly pot, shared by effort

    Each week's pot is 1/8 (12.5%) of the VVAKE the rewards contract holds and hasn't promised yet, at most 1,000,000 VVAKE. It's shared by effort points, at most 150 points a day; nobody gets more than 10% of a week's pot, and what isn't handed out stays in the pool for later weeks. No draw, no chance, never by what you pay, how you entered or what you hold.

  8. 08

    You claim, VVake never holds them

    Every Monday the past week is settled, reviewed by a person, then its list is published on chain. Claims open 48 hours later, so a mistake can be caught before anything moves. You claim from your own wallet. Each week can be claimed for 180 days after its claims open; after that, what wasn't claimed can be swept to VVake's treasury address (fixed in the contract) and can't be claimed any more.

  9. 09

    Testnet only

    Today this runs on Robinhood Chain Testnet: the tokens have no value. Legal review and an external audit come before any mainnet value. Nothing here is an offer, an investment or a promise of anything.

05 / Creators

A share for trainings that move other people

In the beta (build 37) you can build and share your own camera trainings. When other people complete them for real, their creator earns points and, on testnet, a small share of the weekly rewards pool.

  1. Points first

    5 creator points each time another person completes your training with heart data from a watch or a strap, at most 100 a day. They're your points like any other, but they don't add to your effort for the weekly prizes.

  2. 5% of the existing weekly pool

    No new tokens are made for creators. 5% of each week's existing rewards pool is shared by qualifying completions; the other 95% is shared by effort, as before.

  3. At most 2% per creator

    Nobody takes more than 2% of a week's pool. What a capped creator can't take goes to the others; what nobody can take stays in the pool for later weeks.

  4. Held for 7 days

    Each share is held for 7 days after the week ends, so a person can review it and void it in case of fraud. Then it joins the next week's claims, from your own wallet like any other week.

  5. Only real runs count

    A completion counts when it's by another established account (for now: 14 days old with 5 earlier sessions with heart data), with heart data from a watch or a strap (camera-only and web runs never count), and 80% of the work done. Once a person per training per week, never from your own devices, never between blocked people, and not for two accounts that complete each other's trainings.

  6. A testnet pilot

    This runs on Robinhood Chain Testnet: the tokens have no value. The apps show points only; a creator's share shows here once a wallet is connected. Nothing here is an offer, an investment or a promise of anything.
