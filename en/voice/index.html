Voice commands

Say it. VVake does it.

Press the mic and say it your way: VVake understands what you mean, runs it and answers out loud. On the phone or on your Apple Watch. Or just ask Siri: “Hey Siri, ask Wake when is my next training”.

01 / Voice commands

How it works

  1. 01

    Press the mic

    On Today on your phone, on the watch's Today page, or in the workout controls. Nothing is always listening.

  2. 02

    Say it your way

    In English or French, with “Wake” before, after or in the middle, or not at all. No exact phrase to learn: commands are understood on the device, so they work offline.

  3. 03

    VVake runs it and answers

    It shows what it understood, does it and says it back. If two things fit, it asks which one. A question goes to your coach, whose answer you hear; then just answer back.

  4. 04

    Music search

    Say what you want to hear: VVake stops listening after about 2 seconds of silence and searches by itself.

02 / Voice commands

Say it your way

Works with every command: the name anywhere, your own words, several at once.

  • Its name anywhere, or not at all

    “Wake” before, after or in the middle: “Wake, play my music”, “play my music, Wake”, “play, Wake, my music”. It hears “Wake”, “VVake”, “Ouèk” however it’s pronounced, in English or French.

    Say

    • “Wake, play my music”
    • “Play my music, Wake”
    • “Play, Wake, my music”
    • “Hey Wake, skip this song”

    In French

    • « Ouèk, mets ma musique »
    • « Mets ma musique avec Ouèk »
    • « Wake, lance le yoga »
    • « Mets, Wake, de la musique »

  • Say it your way

    No exact words to learn: VVake listens for what you mean. If two things fit, it asks which one.

    Say

    • “Can you put some music on”
    • “I really want to go for a run”
    • “Turn the music off”
    • “Could you skip to the next track”

    In French

    • « Tu peux mettre un peu de musique »
    • « J’veux de la musique »
    • « Coupe le son »
    • « Envoie du son »

  • Every sport by its verb

    Say what you do, the way you say it: “row”, “I’m going to lift weights”, “play tennis”. After “play”, a sport starts the workout; anything else is music.

    Say

    • “Wake, row”
    • “Lift weights”
    • “Let’s play some football”
    • “I’m going to climb”
    • “Stop rowing”

    In French

    • « Ouèk, cours »
    • « On boxe »
    • « On va ramer »
    • « Joue au foot »
    • « Arrête de nager »

  • Several commands at once

    Link them with “and”, “then”: they run in order. A song title with “and” stays one song.

    Say

    • “Wake up and start a run and play Radiohead”
    • “Start a 30 minute run then play music”

    In French

    • « Wake et lance une course puis mets de la musique »
    • « Lance une course et ensuite parle au coach »

03 / Voice commands

With Siri

No setup: these work as soon as VVake is installed, on your iPhone, your Apple Watch and your AirPods. More actions (open a page, your daily move, a ghost challenge, postpone the Claude lock) are in the Shortcuts app.

Say it “Wake”

Say the app’s name “VVake” (“vee wake”) or just “Wake”: “Hey Siri, start a run with VVake”.

In French, Siri also knows VVake as « Vi Ouèk »

  • Vee Wake sounds like “vee wake”
  • Wake sounds like “wake”
  • The Wake sounds like “the wake”

Ask Wake anything

One breath: your next session, your week, your streak, the clash. Or say “Hey Siri, ask Wake”, then anything: a command, or a question for your coach. Siri says the answer.
