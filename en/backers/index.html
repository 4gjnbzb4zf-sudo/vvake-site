01 / 06:00 AM

You remember this feeling.

2022. Your energy just refilled. You lace up, open the app, and go. Around the world, hundreds of thousands of people were doing the same thing at the same moment.

It was the most fun many of us ever had in crypto. Then it broke. Sneakers cost more than real sneakers. Every month there was something new to buy. The people who came to move ended up paying for the people who came to extract.

The ritual was real. The economics weren't. So we rebuilt it from the ground up: we kept the magic and threw out everything that broke it.

What we fixed

  • $1,000 to start→Free to start. No NFT paywall, ever.
  • Rewards printed without limit→Rewards only from real revenue, never from new users.
  • Speed decided everything→Your effort decides: your heart on your own zones, plus GPS or your health sensors.
  • A new thing to buy every month→One ecosystem. No pay-more-for-less treadmill.

Why a claim, not the launchpad airdrop

For fairness. vibe/vibe's Airdrop Board tokens stay locked until the token "graduates", and stay locked forever if it never does, and they go to a list without checking who really bought. Instead, wallets that actually bought the old $VVAKE through its pool get a one-time claim from a published snapshot: a pinned block, team and contract wallets excluded, dust filtered out, suspected farm wallets reviewed. Each wallet claims in proportion to what it bought, and only once, enforced by the contract, so nobody is paid twice and farmers don't dilute real buyers. The snapshot list is published before claims open.

🏆 VVake Coach Plus funds a monthly prize pool

Each month 15% of net VVake Coach Plus revenue (store proceeds after Apple's or Google's commission and withheld taxes, minus refunds) goes to the prize pool, a public contract (VVakePrizePool), with a hash of that month's report on chain. The pool turns it into $VVAKE by rule, never by anyone's choice.

  1. 1.Conversions run in small capped chunks, at most once a day, and anyone can trigger the next one
  2. 2.The $VVAKE goes only to the rewards contract (VVakeRewards), never to a person or a team wallet
  3. 3.Each week, prizes for effort. Two ways in, same prize: VVake Coach Plus or the free entry (no purchase necessary), with an account, an entry and a linked wallet (18+) from before the week, at least 3 heart-rate sessions or 90 active minutes recorded with the app on a real iPhone or Apple Watch, and the week's skill question. Never by holdings, never by draw
  4. 4.The week's pot is 1/8 (12.5%) of the $VVAKE the rewards contract holds and hasn't promised yet, at most 1,000,000. It's shared by effort points (at most 150 a day), nobody gets more than 10%, and what isn't handed out stays in the pool for later weeks. You claim from your own wallet on vvake.com/rewards
  5. 5.During the pilot: at most 1,000 VVAKE per person and 20,000 VVAKE in all each week, for the first 200 eligible people; others join a waiting list. What a cap leaves stays in the pool for later weeks. A person reviews every week, and nothing is posted until that review is done.

Testnet today: the tokens have no value. Nothing is burned and nothing goes to holders. Legal review and an external audit of the contracts come before any mainnet value. Paying VVake Coach Plus with testnet $VVAKE exists only in development builds of the app, to test the flow (the tokens go to the VVake treasury); it isn't offered in TestFlight or the App Store.
