Drop 2 · free v1 holders

Sent: all 3,199 wallets are paid. The team paid the gas.

Basis = what the v1 launch airdrop and the launchpad's holder share sent each wallet. Pro rata on 40 % of the creator wallet's remaining reserve (about 25 % of the 1:1 amount), capped at 10 % per wallet; 722 wallets under 1,000 left out; the 74 buyers of drop 1 aren't in it. The team sends every wallet its amount and pays the gas.

  • →These wallets got v1 for free, from the launch airdrop or the launchpad's holder share. They never bought; the buyers were paid in drop 1.
  • →Paying them 1:1 would have needed more tokens than the team has. The team chose not to buy the rest on the market: that would have pushed the price up about 3.7× and drained about half of the pool, at everyone else's expense.
  • →So they share 40 % of what the creator wallet had left, pro rata: about 25 % of what v1 sent each. 722 wallets that would have got under 1,000 are left out.

The creator wallet keeps 92,910,731 $VVAKE for community events.
