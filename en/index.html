01 / How it works

Less planning. More showing up.

You choose the goal. VVake helps you find a rhythm you can keep: plan, move, see progress.

  1. 01Make it yours

    Make a plan

    Tell your coach your goal, your free days and how long you have. It builds your week around your calendar and goes lighter after a tough one.

  2. 02Get out there

    Move at your pace

    Start on your iPhone or Apple Watch. Heart rate, zone and route live; your coach checks in by voice if you opt in. Any sport counts.

  3. 03Feel the momentum

    See your progress

    Your week and your journal in one place, and your VVaker grows as you move. Scored on effort for your heart, not on speed.

Questions

Can I use VVake now?+

Not yet. The iPhone and Apple Watch apps are in a private beta with a small group of testers, not in the App Store. The Android phone and Wear OS apps are in development (see the device status above). Join early access and we'll email you when you can start.

When does it launch in my city?+

City by city: a city goes live once enough people join, and rival cities go live together. There's no date yet. If your city isn't on the list, request it when you sign up: the most requested cities come next.

How do I set the app up?+

With a first conversation with your coach, by voice or typed, free. The coach leads, one short question at a time, with examples when you're not sure, then proposes your plan, reminders, habits, food if you want it, a crew and the screens you need. Nothing changes until you confirm, and you can undo it all within 30 days. Prefer tapping? The cards are always there. In the beta from build 73, testers first. More →

Does the coach cut me off?+

It shouldn't anymore. From build 74 the coach waits for you to finish: short pauses pass, an unfinished sentence gets more time, and "wait" or "hold on" makes it stop and stay quiet until you're back. After a question it checks in once if you need a moment, then waits. Set its patience to Quick, Normal or Relaxed in Settings › Coach (it learns how you talk), or turn on Hold to talk. Testers first. More →

Can I talk while the coach is talking?+

Yes, from build 77. Calls are a real conversation: the coach keeps listening while it talks. Talk over it and it turns its voice down at once, then stops and listens if you go on; a quick "mm-hm", "yeah" or "ok" and it carries on. Words you say over it are kept and answered, never cut. On the web, the sound goes for transcription only while you speak, and nothing is recorded. More →

Can I follow a YouTube workout and still get my reps counted?+

Yes, from build 74 (testers first). Paste a YouTube or Vimeo link: VVake reads the title, chapters and description to propose the moves (never the video itself), you check the list, and the video plays in its official player next to your camera while VVake counts. No list? Free count counts any move you repeat. Every video you follow stays in My videos (star your favourites, replay in a tap), and importing your YouTube playlists is coming soon. Use any camera, or show it all on a TV with the big screen. More →

Can VVake import my private YouTube playlists or Liked videos?+

Soon, from build 75 once we switch it on (testers first). Link YouTube in My videos or Settings › Connections: VVake asks Google for read-only access, separate from Google Calendar, and you pick which playlists (private ones and Liked videos too) to bring into My videos. VVake reads only list names, counts and video titles, never shares them and never puts them in the shared library. Unlink any time: Google's access is revoked and the videos you haven't done are removed. While Google reviews VVake's access, its consent page may show a warning. More →

Do I need to be fit, or a runner?+

No. Walking, cycling, swimming, strength and yoga all count, at your pace. Your plan starts from where you are: your days, your minutes, your level. Sessions are scored on effort for your heart, not speed, and if something hurts the plan goes lighter and low-impact. VVake isn't medical advice: check with a doctor if in doubt.

Does VVake adapt to me?+

Yes, and never behind your back. VVake learns from your sessions (and from Apple Health or Health Connect if you share it): how rested you are, the paces and heart rates that suit you, and how you're tracking toward your goals, shown in Your progress with the reason for every change. When something should change (a lighter week, new heart zones, a later date for a goal), it proposes it: you accept or say not now, and you can undo for 30 days. During a planned session it reacts to where you are, not to a fixed clock: a rest ends when your heart has come down, a rep stops early when it's too much or gets a little longer when you're fresh. Prefer the plan as written? Choose Fixed timing in your settings. Until it has a few weeks of your data it says it's still learning instead of guessing. In the beta from build 62.

Can I start over after a break?+

Yes. Fresh start (Settings, on the phone or the watch) resets your plan and schedule, your streak, your daily habits, your custom health settings (heart zones, resting and max heart rate), focus lock, the coach's memory and what VVake learned about your training (the daily Health numbers it kept and your progress, which it starts learning again), so you begin again at day 1. Your VVaker, your sessions, your scores, points and prizes, your wallet, VVake Coach Plus, your friends and your Apple Health or Health Connect data all stay: nothing is deleted from them. You hold the button to confirm, so it never happens by accident.

Do I need a watch to earn points?+

No. A walk, run, hike or ride with just your phone earns about 80% of the points the same workout would earn with heart rate, streak bonus included, when the phone's checks agree (steps that fit the distance, no stretch in a vehicle, the real app). With a watch or a heart strap, a workout can also count toward the weekly prizes; phone-only workouts never do. Workouts in the web app keep their own rule: points with a heart-rate strap, never prizes. More →

Does paying get me a better rank or more prizes?+

No. VVake Coach Plus and VVake Coach Premium let walks and runs with just your phone count more points a day for your own progress (60 free, 100 with Plus, 150 with Premium): your levels, your VVaker, your journey. Leagues, challenges and leaderboards count those points up to 60 a day for everyone, and the weekly prizes follow heart-rate effort only, the same on every plan. Money never buys a better rank or a share of the prizes. More →

Is it free? Do I need a wallet or crypto?+

Free to start, and you never need a wallet or crypto to train. A wallet is only for claiming the optional weekly $VVAKE prizes, on vvake.com/rewards, never in the app. Optional extras like VVake Coach Plus are never required to train or take part in challenges and never give an edge in them; the weekly $VVAKE prizes are open with VVake Coach Plus or the free entry (same prize, no purchase necessary), with a linked wallet (18+). More →

What happens to my data?+

For early access we keep your email, your city, your language and the optional answers you give (team, wearable). In the app you share health data field by field; it's never sold and never used for ads. The apps also count which features are used (never what you do in them), on our own servers, to improve VVake; you can turn that off any time. More →

Is there a token? What are the $VVAKE prizes?+

VVake is a fitness and training service, not a financial product. Each week, people who moved enough with heart-rate data (at least 3 sessions of 10+ minutes or 90 active minutes, with movement) share $VVAKE prizes by effort. Two ways in, same prize: VVake Coach Plus or the free entry (no purchase necessary), with an account, an entry and a linked wallet from before the week, and a short weekly skill question. Only sessions recorded with the VVake app on a real iPhone or Apple Watch count, and during the pilot prizes are capped and reviewed by a person every week. Prizes are for people 18 or older, points count up to 150 a day, nobody gets more than 10% of a week's pot, and prizes never depend on chance, on how much you pay or on how many tokens you hold. 15% of net VVake Coach Plus revenue funds the prize pool, converted on chain by public rules. You claim from your own wallet on vvake.com/rewards; VVake never holds your tokens and never asks for your recovery phrase. Testnet today: the tokens have no value, nothing to buy, and nothing here is an offer or investment advice. Not offered in the US or UK at launch, subject to legal review. More →
