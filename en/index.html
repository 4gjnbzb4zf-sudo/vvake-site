Questions

Can I use VVake now?+

Not yet in the stores. The iPhone, Android, Apple Watch and Wear OS apps are in a private beta with invited testers. Join early access and we'll email you when you can start, or try the web app now.

When does it launch in my city?+

City by city: a city goes live once enough people join, and rival cities go live together. No date yet. Not on the list? Request your city when you sign up.

Do I need to be fit, or a runner?+

No. Walking, cycling, swimming, strength and yoga all count, at your pace, scored on effort for your heart, not speed. VVake isn't medical advice: check with a doctor if in doubt.

Can I really talk with the coach?+

Yes. Call Maya or Leo, or talk from the Coach tab: it waits for you to finish and you can talk over it. Live minutes during workouts come with VVake Coach Plus and Premium. More →

Do I need a watch to earn points?+

No. A walk, run, hike or ride with just your phone earns about 80% of the points it would with heart rate. With a watch or a heart strap, a workout can also count toward the weekly prizes. More →

Does paying get me a better rank or more prizes?+

No. Leagues, challenges and leaderboards count the same for everyone, and prizes follow heart-rate effort only. Plans add coach time, never an edge. More →

Is it free? Do I need a wallet or crypto?+

Free to start, and you never need a wallet or crypto to train. A wallet is only for claiming the optional weekly $VVAKE prizes on vvake.com/rewards, never in the app. More →

What happens to my data?+

You share health data field by field; it's never sold and never used for ads. Camera video stays on your device. Feature counts help us improve VVake, and you can turn them off. More →

Is there a token? What are the $VVAKE prizes?+

VVake is a fitness service, not a financial product. Each week, people who moved enough with heart-rate data share $VVAKE prizes by effort, with VVake Coach Plus or the free entry (same prize, no purchase necessary), 18+. Testnet today: the tokens have no value and there is nothing to buy. Nothing here is an offer or investment advice. More →
