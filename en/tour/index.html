01 / Coach

A coach you can talk to.

Maya or Leo sets the app up with you, calls when it matters and keeps you company mid-run.

  • Set up by talking

    One short chat sets your plan, reminders and habits. You confirm before anything changes.

  • Live calls

    Call your coach any time and talk while you move. Live minutes come with Plus and Premium.

  • It lets you finish

    It waits for your whole sentence, and you can talk over it.

  • One Coach tab

    Talk first; your plan, the coach's memory and its proposals right below.

  • Proposes, never forces

    Zones, reminders or goals: it suggests, you approve, Undo is one tap.

  • Over your music

    Its voice plays over Spotify with a light duck, on the phone or the watch.

03 / Beat the clock

Pick a time. Hold the pace.

Walk, run or hike a distance in a time you pick, with a target runner and your coach on pace.

  • A target that fits

    A time suggested from your own sessions, and the pace it needs.

  • Your pace plan

    Even, easy start or negative split.

  • Ahead or behind, live

    Projected finish and the pace to hold, by voice over your music.

  • Care first

    Heart rate too high? It suggests easing off. Drop the target in one tap.

  • Your result

    Splits against the plan, new bests, retry or a kinder next target.

  • Everywhere

    iPhone, Android, Apple Watch, Wear OS and the web app.

Points like any session, no prize of its own.

04 / Train

Any sport. Any camera. Any video.

Every sport counts on effort for your heart, and the camera counts the reps for you.

  • Camera workouts

    Prop the phone 2 to 3 m away: reps, holds and form cues. The video stays on your device.

  • Follow a video

    Paste a YouTube or Vimeo link and VVake counts the moves along with it.

  • Any camera, big screen

    Webcam, USB camera or your iPhone in the browser, and the session on your TV.

  • Your own moves

    Teach VVake a move in 3 to 5 reps, build sets with heart zones, share them by link.

  • Season plans

    Training for a 10K or an Ironman? Your coach plans the months ahead in phases.

  • A week that adapts

    Your plan fits your calendar, eases off after a hard week and stays editable.

05 / Nutrition · optional

Eat for how you train.

Snap a meal, plan the week, shop from one list.

  • From a photo

    Take a picture and VVake fills in the meal. Adjust quantities in a tap.

  • Meal plans

    A week of meals fitted to your training, with your allergies always respected.

  • One grocery list

    Everything for the week in one list you can share at home.

  • In your calendar

    Meals and prep land in your calendar, with the cooking steps.

  • Cook with what you have

    A photo of your fridge gives three recipe ideas (Premium).

  • Plans that follow you

    Ate something else? Log it and it takes the planned meal's place.

Health first: gentle targets, no food is ever judged.

07 / Devices

Your phone, your watch, any brand.

iPhone or Android, Apple Watch or Wear OS, or just a browser.

  • Apple Watch and Wear OS

    Start, pause and follow your zones on the wrist, round or square.

  • Mix brands

    Phone and watch stay in sync through VVake, even across brands.

  • Spotify on the watch

    Your music plays on the device you pick (Spotify Premium).

  • Heart-rate straps

    Pair any Bluetooth chest strap or arm band.

  • Health apps

    Apple Health and Health Connect, field by field, only if you share.

  • Web app

    vvake.com/app: GPS and heart-rate workouts in the browser, no install.

08 / The real app

Not a mockup. The app, running.

The iPhone and Apple Watch apps: your plan, your goal, racing your best, the daily rhythm and the focus lock.

  • Today

    Your VVaker, today's plan, your goal and the coach's check-in.

  • Your goal

    Be consistent, X sessions in X weeks, a distance, or (adults only) a weight goal held to a safe pace: 0.5 kg a week at most, never below a healthy weight.

  • Your plan

    Fits your week and your body: heart-rate targets from the health data you share.

  • Live, on one screen

    Your heart, the map with your route, a zone block counting down, a hands-free coach one tap away and your music. Race your best and your past self runs there too.

  • Daily rhythm

    Water, meals and sleep around your training. No calorie counting. Answer right on the reminder, on your phone or watch: Done, In 15 min or Not this time. A daily habit score, points and trophies.

  • Focus lock

    For builders: after a long desk stretch, your Mac locks until you move.

  • Your health data

    Share only what you want, field by field. Never sold.

  • Join the Rally

    Your Brand Team moved: pick any sport and sweat it out together.

  • You

    Your VVaker, your week and your journal.

  • Start in one tap

    Today's plan, or race your best.

  • Your route

09 / Fair play

Only real effort counts.

Points come from your heart and your steps, never from a car or a card.

  • Effort, not speed

    Time in heart-rate zones becomes points, so every sport and level counts.

  • No car rides

    A stretch that moves like a vehicle doesn't score.

  • Counted once

    One walk shared across several accounts counts once.

  • No paid edge

    Plans never buy a better rank or a bigger prize share.

  • Health first

    Zone 5 never scores more than zone 4. Rest days are part of the plan.

  • Prizes, honestly

    Weekly $VVAKE prizes run on testnet today: no value, nothing to buy.
