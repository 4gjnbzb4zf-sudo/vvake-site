01 / Set up with your coach

Your first conversation sets the app up around you.

No form to fill in. Your coach introduces themself and leads the way, one short question at a time: what's got you moving, what you'd like help with, how your week looks. Not sure what to say? Tap one of the examples. Then the coach proposes a setup, and nothing changes until you confirm it.

  1. 1

    Meet your coach

    Two coaches, Maya and Leo: hear each voice first, then tap Call. This first meetup is a short live call, free: your coach says hi and asks what you're after, in your own words. Rather type, or tap the cards? Both are right under it.

  2. 2

    Talk it through

    Your goals, your sports, your days and minutes, where you train, what you'd like help with. Say a lot or a little: the coach gives examples when you hesitate and leaves room when you have more to say.

  3. 3

    See the proposal

    Everything the coach suggests is listed with the real values (which days, how long, what time) and its own switch. Turn off anything you don't want.

  4. 4

    Confirm, and it's ready

    One tap sets it all up and shows what changed and your first thing to try. Changed your mind? Undo it all in one tap within 30 days.

What it can set up

  • ✓Your week's plan, or a season plan for an event
  • ✓Reminders at the times you pick
  • ✓Daily habits: water, meals, sleep
  • ✓Nutrition, only if you want it
  • ✓A crew to train with, or a friend to invite
  • ✓The screens you need, the rest hidden
  • ✓Your coach's voice and style
  • ✓A first thing to try today

If you have a sport watch, the coach helps you connect it. If you don't, it may say heart rate and effort follow-up are better with one, and that your phone works fine: no brand, no link, no sales pitch.

Along the way your coach gets to know you: what you do for fun, the food you love, how you unwind. Only if you share it, and you see, edit or delete it any time under About you.

Later, Tailor my app is a catch-up: your coach remembers what you said and asks what changed, then shows only the differences. A voice catch-up uses your live coach minutes (the card shows how many are left this week); typing or the cards cost nothing. When voice isn't available the card says why and when it opens again.

02 / Talk like with a person

Your coach lets you finish.

Pause to breathe, to think, to find the word: the coach waits. It hears when a sentence isn't over ("and then…", "because…"), says a soft "mm-hm" when you're telling a long story, and only answers when you're done, in a sentence or two with one question at a time.

  1. 1

    It waits for you

    Short pauses pass. An unfinished sentence gets more time. Out of breath mid-workout? It waits longer and never talks over you.

  2. 2

    Say "wait"

    "Wait", "hold on", "give me a sec": the coach stops and stays quiet until you're back. "I'm busy" or "later": it says it'll pick this up later and remembers where you were.

  3. 3

    Still thinking?

    After a question, if you need a moment, it gently checks in once ("Take your time. Still thinking?"), then waits without pushing.

  4. 4

    Your pace

    Patience: Quick, Normal or Relaxed, and it learns how you talk. Prefer walkie-talkie? Turn on Hold to talk: it answers when you let go.

A real conversation

From build 77 your coach keeps listening while it talks, like a person on the phone. Talk over it and it turns its voice down at once, then stops and listens if you go on; a quick "mm-hm", "yeah" or "ok" and it carries on. What you say over it, or right as it stops, is kept and answered: no more cut words. Your phone's or browser's echo cancellation keeps the coach's own voice out of what it hears; on a loud speaker, headphones help most.

Get to know me

Want your coach to know you better? Tap Get to know me: it asks a few warm questions, one at a time (what got you moving, what you do for fun, the food you love, how you unwind), never about your weight, your body or your health conditions. Your answers go under About you in Coach memory, where you edit or delete them any time.

03 / Any camera, any screen

Train with any camera. Follow any video.

Your camera workout now works with the camera you have and the screen you like: a webcam, an external camera, your phone as the camera with the TV as the screen, and a workout video you follow while VVake counts.

  1. 1

    Pick a camera

    In the web app, any camera your browser shows: the built-in webcam, a USB camera, and on a Mac your iPhone through Continuity Camera. On Android phones, an external camera when the phone supports it.

  2. 2

    Show it on a big screen

    Your phone counts, the TV or laptop shows your reps, timer, move, tips and heart rate, big enough to read from the floor. Open vvake.com/app/screen, type the 6-digit code or scan the QR code. Or the other way round: the laptop counts and your phone sends your heart rate.

  3. 3

    Follow a video

    Paste a YouTube or Vimeo link. VVake reads the title, chapters and description (never the video itself) to propose the moves, you check the list, then the video plays in its official player next to the camera while VVake counts your reps. Pause the video and the counting pauses too.

  4. 4

    No list? Free count

    VVake counts any move you repeat and names it if it knows it. After the set, name it once and it counts it next time.

Good to know

  • ✓Chill mode: no score or countdown beeps during the session, calmer cues, longer rests
  • ✓Video routines are saved private, in your routines library
  • ✓My videos keeps every video you followed with your move list: star favourites, replay one in a tap
  • ✓Coming soon, once VVake's server switches it on: import a YouTube playlist into My videos (public or unlisted, or your private ones and Liked videos by linking YouTube, read only), and re-sync it when you add videos
  • ✓Share one to the library and the video's creator is credited
  • ✓Same points as any camera workout, same daily caps
  • ✓The camera picture never leaves your device

In the beta from build 74, testers first. The screen link ends after 2 hours.

06 / The app

Built for your wrist. Made for your crew.

Today's session first, started in one tap. Your watch runs the session, your phone tells the story: today's plan, your squad and your city's clash in one place.

VVAKE UP VVAKE UP
  1. 01

    Training first, one tap

    Today opens on today's planned session: Start in one tap, or race your best. The watch's Today starts the same way, and Siri can start any sport.

  2. 02

    Zones in your own numbers

    “Zone 2 · 112–128 bpm” from your Health profile, plus the pace or speed that should put you there, learned from your sessions. On steady days, zone blocks count down (3 minutes on a run, 4 on a walk): hold your zone 80% of the block and it earns 10% more. Set your own zones per sport if you know them. No heart rate? Speed zones you set per sport guide you and the coach's cues instead: on the web, and in the app since build 28 for testers (points still need a heart rate).

  3. 03

    Zones for every sport

    Your heart tops out lower on a bike than on a run, and lower again in the pool or when only your arms work, so each sport gets its own heart-rate zones, worked out for you. Know your max for a sport? Set it, or set your own zones in bpm or percent. Pace reads the way the sport does: per km or mile on foot, km/h or mph on wheels, per 100 m in the water, per 500 m on a rower, and not at all where pace means nothing. Prefer effort by feel? One switch shows easy, steady and hard instead of numbers on every device. Set it on the web or on your account page now, and in the app from the next beta build. Wellness guidance, not medical advice; points and prizes don't change.

  4. 04

    Play on: phone or watch

    Choose where Spotify, Apple Music or a VVake mix plays: your iPhone, your Apple Watch or a speaker, and Spotify starts right there, never somewhere else. Spotify asleep? One tap opens it and comes straight back. Nothing playing? Your last song shows, one tap plays it. A VVake mix moves between watch and phone at the same track and second.

  5. 05

    Maps that keep up

    Street-level maps on phone and watch, the ghost and the gap above the controls, and your watch's workout on one phone screen: map, heart rate, coach and music, with your route drawn as you go. On the watch, pick Standard, City, Outdoor or Satellite. Routes pass between your own watch and phone, so route ghosts work on both.

  6. 06

    Never lose the session

    A workout keeps going behind other screens: a “Back to your workout” pill brings you back. It ends on the session's details with your GPS path, the points and why they count.

  7. 07

    Apple's workout engine

    The watch records with Apple's own workout session (HealthKit). Battery use depends on GPS, music and the screen.

07 / How it works

Short daily sessions. Real momentum.

  • Energy refills on your clock

    Energy refills four times a day in your local time zone. One energy is five minutes of real effort. Show up, spend it, come back.

  • Heart, not speed

    Effort is scored from your own heart-rate zones, set from your age and resting heart rate, with a bonus for beating your own baseline. A brisk walk can count as much as a run. No watch? A walk or run with just your phone earns about 80% of the same workout's points.

  • Squads and rivalries

    Team up in squads, pass the baton across time zones, and put your city on the board in 12-hour clashes.

  • Works anywhere

    Apple Watch or iPhone (Android and Wear OS are in development). No signal on the trail? Sessions record offline and sync later, so you never lose a day.

Camera workout (phone and web)

In the beta now (build 51, testers)

Prop your phone 2 to 3 m away: VVake counts your reps and holds for bodyweight and weight training (you enter the load), gives form cues by voice, and your VVaker moves with you. The video never leaves your phone and nothing is recorded. Without a heart sensor it earns small points (at most 15 a day); wear your watch and the watch and the camera make one session with the normal heart points, or pair a Bluetooth heart-rate strap with the phone for heart points too. Never prizes from the camera. No phone? The same camera workout runs in your browser at vvake.com/app with your computer's webcam: the pose is read in the tab, the video never leaves it, and its small points share the same 15 a day.

Your own moves and trainings

In the browser now · in the apps in the beta (build 51, testers)

Teach VVake a move of your own: 3 to 5 reps in front of the camera, and it counts that move from then on, in your sets and in Just start moving. It keeps numbers only (joint angles and timing), never a video or a picture. Build a training from VVake's moves and yours: reps or a hold, a target heart zone (or an effort out of 10 without a sensor) and the rest after each set. Share it with a link, challenge friends to beat your score on it, or, in the apps, show off a brag card with how many people trained it. Find other people's trainings and coaches in Discover. When other people complete your training for real, with heart data from a watch or a strap, you earn creator points.
